HOLOGRID

A 3D holographic engine hangs in the air. Pinch a part to pull it out. Pinch empty space and move to spin the engine. Pinch with both hands to zoom. Put every part back and it fires up.

Hand tracking needs camera permission and downloads a small model. If it is blocked, touch controls still work.
